Text-to-SQL and similar ideas have been repeatedly tried and abandoned across every tech cycle.

* **1990s:** OLAP Cubes naively pre-aggregated data cubes ignored both Finance and Sales definitions.
* **2000s:** Semantics BI introduced code-governed semantics (LookML) but the cost of maintaining endless YAML files exceeded hiring analysts to write manual SQL.
* **2010s:** Qlik associative engine and ThoughtSpot search-driven BI still required massive effort to build and maintain the semantics layer.
* **2020s:** Chat-to-Analyze AI generated flawless SQLs but missed or hallucinated business logic, producing conflicting cohorts and numbers that Finance and Sales refused to trust.

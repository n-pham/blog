# Naive Questions Have Their Purpose

Through our working years, naive questions should not disappear, they should actually increase, changing purpose over time.

* 0-5

  Essential for ourselves to know clearly how things work.

* 5-10

  Naturally avoided, for fear of looking inexperienced.

* 10-15

  They return once we value work clarity over ego.

* 15-20

  Asked for the benefit of junior members who are hesitant to ask.

* 20-25

  To verify the speaker's assumptions.

* 25+

  For a quick smoke check.

# Data Mesh Data Product Lessons Learned

* avoid bottlenecks: make data clean and available - not to become "single-source-of-truth"
* avoid department tightly-coupling, lock-in: provide domain-neutral, self-explanatory fields - not to amplify metric drift
* avoid micro-policies: provide automatic schema contracts - not to enforce top-down "data governance"

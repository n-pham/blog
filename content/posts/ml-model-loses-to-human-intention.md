# ML Model loses to Human Intention

ML models are great at predicting outcomes based on historical patterns, but they fail when human intention and game theory override those patterns:

* Historical data doesn't capture strategic incentives
* "Winning" the match is not always the primary objective
* Mutual benefit (incentive alignment) creates patterns that defy statistics
* Game theory shifts the "goal" in real-time

**Scenario: Portugal vs. Colombia**
* **ML Prediction:** Portugal wins.
* **Human Intention:**
    * A draw gives Portugal 5 points and Colombia 7 points.
    * Their competitors having 0 and 1 point play each other, meaning only one can have maximum 4 points.
    * Actual: 0-0 since both teams did not want to attack.
* **Outcome:** The prediction "Portugal wins" is defeated by the strategic incentive of a draw.
